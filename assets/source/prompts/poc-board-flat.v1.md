## Prompt

Top-down flat graphic design of a premium minimalist Ludo board on a white background, like a high-end printed art board. Four corner home bases, each made of concentric rounded squares stepping in tone from a confident outer colour to near-white at the centre: crimson red (#C8102E), royal blue (#1F5FD6), emerald green (#16B060), golden yellow (#F5BE00). A crisp white cross-shaped track of small square cells with hairline grey separators; the track bends around the bases with softly rounded inner corners. Each colour's home lane runs along the middle of its arm as five cells graduating from pastel at the outer edge to saturated near the centre. A small square centre made of four flat triangles in the four colours. Generous whitespace, perfect alignment, calm and modern, Scandinavian design sensibility. Flat colour, no gradients except the stepped tones, no texture.

## Avoid

Pieces, dice, hands, people, text, numbers, logos, 3D perspective, shadows, wood, marble, noise texture, glitter, neon, bloom, busy patterns, cartoon style, extra or missing arms.

## Acceptance

Reads as Ludo at a glance; the stepped tonal bases feel premium; colours match the hex values closely enough to judge harmony; nothing in it is used as geometry.
