# poc-token-family · v1

- **Purpose:** one token silhouette in four colourways, to approve the pawn shape and resin material
- **Model:** `gpt_image_2_5` · variant `flare` · quality `high` · resolution `2k` · aspect `3:2` · outputs 4
- **Status:** draft, not submitted

## Prompt

Product photograph of four identical premium game pawns standing in a row on a seamless warm off-white sweep (#F3F0EA). Each pawn: wide stable round base with a softly bevelled edge, a slim concave waist, and a smooth domed head with a small flat top facet. Proportions: base diameter 0.8, height 0.95. Material: polished coloured resin with a clear glossy coat and soft depth, opaque (not glass), with a darker smoked ring around the base. Colours, left to right: crimson #C8102E, royal blue #1F5FD6, emerald #16B060, golden yellow #F5BE00. A small simple symbol is inlaid flush on each flat top facet: a heart, a water drop, a leaf, a lightning bolt, in white or near-black for contrast. Three-quarter view from slightly above, soft key light from the upper left, gentle contact shadows, crisp focus on all four.

## Avoid

Faces, eyes, chess-piece shapes, crowns, metallic chrome, transparency, glitter, text, logos, props, hands, dramatic shadows, bloom, background patterns.

## Acceptance

One consistent silhouette across all four; readable as a Ludo pawn; resin material; colours match; symbols legible. A good candidate becomes the reference image for the 3D comparison job.
