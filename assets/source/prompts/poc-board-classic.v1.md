## Prompt

Premium modern Ludo board game photographed from directly above on a soft warm-grey studio surface. A square board with a satin lacquered frame in warm ivory (#ECE6DC) and a crisp grid of matte porcelain cells (#F7F4EE) separated by fine hairline grooves. The traditional cross-shaped track; four corner home bases in crimson (#C8102E), royal blue (#1F5FD6), emerald (#16B060) and golden yellow (#F5BE00), each an inset tray with four shallow cups. Home lanes in softened tints of the same colours. The centre is a shallow four-sided pyramid in the four colours. Polished resin pawns sit in some cups and on a few track cells: glossy, with a dark smoked base ring. Soft single key light from the upper left, gentle contact shadows, subtle controlled reflections on the pawns only. Clean, contemporary, playful but premium, like a design-store edition of a classic game.

## Avoid

Text, numbers, logos, people, hands, dice clutter, wood grain on the playing surface, marble, heavy textures or patterns on cells, neon, bloom, lens flare, dramatic dark mood, cartoon style, fish-eye distortion, extra or missing arms of the cross.

## Acceptance

Light, readable cells; colours recognisably match the hex values; materials read as lacquer, porcelain and resin; nothing that we would copy as geometry.
