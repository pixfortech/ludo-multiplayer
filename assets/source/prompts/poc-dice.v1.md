# poc-dice · v1

- **Purpose:** premium die concept (material, edge rounding, pip treatment, six detail)
- **Model:** `gpt_image_2_5` · variant `flare` · quality `high` · resolution `2k` · aspect `3:2` · outputs 4
- **Status:** draft, deferred to a later batch (not part of the 15-credit proof of concept)

## Prompt

Product photograph of a single premium six-sided die shown three times on a seamless warm off-white sweep (#F3F0EA): resting showing the six face, tilted showing three faces, and from above. Rounded cube with generous soft edges (edge radius about 18% of the side). Material: ivory resin (#F6F1E7), satin-gloss clear coat, subtle depth. Pips are cleanly recessed circles filled near-black (#141821). On the six face only, each pip has a fine gold inlay ring (#E3B341). Standard die layout, opposite faces sum to seven. Soft key light from the upper left, gentle contact shadows.

## Avoid

Casino red dice, translucent or glass dice, metallic or chrome, engraved text, logos, numbers instead of pips, wear and scratches, hands, motion blur, busy backgrounds, bloom.

## Acceptance

Correct pip layouts; edges soft but still clearly a cube; ivory reads as premium resin; the gold detail is subtle.
