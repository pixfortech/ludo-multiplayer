# poc-environment-tabletop · v1

- **Purpose:** "luxury tabletop" environment backdrop reference (the board itself is rendered by the game)
- **Model:** `gpt_image_2_5` · variant `flare` · quality `high` · resolution `2k` · aspect `16:9` · outputs 4
- **Status:** draft, not submitted

## Prompt

Empty premium game table viewed at a gentle three-quarter angle, ready for a board game to be placed in the centre. A large clear central area of smooth deep-green wool felt framed by a warm dark walnut table edge with a soft satin finish. The room behind is softly out of focus: warm neutral walls and a hint of a modern lamp, calm and uncluttered. Soft, even light from the upper left, a gentle pool of light on the felt centre, no harsh shadows. Calm, sophisticated, inviting.

## Avoid

Any board, cards, dice or game pieces; people; text; logos; strong colours competing with player colours; dark moody low-key lighting; bloom; lens flare; busy patterns.

## Acceptance

The centre area is clean and large enough for a square board; the light is neutral; the image works dimmed behind a light board.
